Recording a route

On Home, tap Start and allow location access. The app records until you tap Stop, including with the screen locked. Points are saved on your phone first and upload when you're online.

A route has gaps or stops early? Battery savers, turning off location, force-closing the app or restarting the phone can pause recording. Open the app again to resume. On Android, allow the app to run without battery restrictions; on iPhone, choose "Always" location access in Settings.

Seeing a day from another phone

Sign in with the same account, pick the day, and the app downloads that day's uploaded route.

Chats and calls

Find people by name or username in Chats. To stop someone contacting you, open the chat's info and tap Block. To report a message, touch and hold it and choose Report; to report a person, use Report in the chat's info. We review reports and remove content or accounts that break the Terms of Use.
