Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

My Journey ("the app") is a route recorder with chat and calls for Android and iPhone. It is provided by Rakesh Kumar ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. Contact: softwaredevrk@gmail.com.

In short: we collect only what the app needs to work. Your location is recorded only between Start and Stop. We do not show ads, do not track you across other apps or websites, and do not sell or share your data for marketing. You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Account details: email address, first and last name, username, and (optional) phone numberTo create your account, sign you in, and let people find you by name or username in chat. Your email and phone are never shown in search.
PasswordTo sign you in. It is stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt); nobody, including us, can read it.
Precise location (time, position, accuracy and speed of each recorded point)To record and show your route. Recording happens only after you tap Start and ends when you tap Stop. The phone shows that recording is on (the iPhone location indicator, or the Android notification with a Stop button).
Profile photo (optional)To show on your profile, in chats and on your map pin. The app shrinks the photo and removes its metadata (such as where it was taken) before upload.
Chat content: messages, photos, voice messages, shared locations and routes, reactionsTo deliver your messages to the people in your chats.
Call records: who called whom, when, and for how longTo show call history in your chats. The audio, video and shared screen of a call are never stored or sent through our servers.
Safety records: people you block, reports you make (with a copy of the reported message)To keep blocked people away from you and to review abuse reports.
Device and technical data: app version, permission states (for example, whether location access is allowed), a sign-in credential per device, push notification tokens, the time and IP address of your last sign-inTo keep you signed in securely, send notifications, troubleshoot problems and protect the service from abuse.
Terms acceptance: which version of the Terms of Use you accepted, and whenTo record that you agreed to the Terms of Use.

We do not collect contacts, browsing history, health, financial or advertising data. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

2. Permissions the app asks for

  • Location: to record your route while you record, and to show where you are on the map.
  • Microphone: for voice messages (only while you hold the record button) and for calls.
  • Camera: for video calls, only when you turn your camera on.
  • Notifications: for new messages, incoming calls and the recording notice.
  • Photos: only the photo you pick, through the system picker.

You can change these at any time in your phone's settings.

3. Who can see your data

  • You can see your own routes, profile and chats.
  • People you chat with see the messages, photos, voice messages, locations and routes you send them, your name, username and photo, and, if you both allow it, when you were last online.
  • Managers. If your organisation gives someone the manager role, that person can see every account's details, profile photo and uploaded routes. Managers cannot see chats or calls. Every time a manager opens a list or a person's day, it is recorded in an access log.
  • Calls connect your phone directly to the other person's phone, so each phone learns the other's IP address during a call. Only people you have a chat with can call you, and you can turn calls off.
  • Us (the operator). Messages are encrypted in transit but not end-to-end encrypted, so we can technically access them. We only do so to operate the service, review abuse reports, or when required by law.

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf or to provide their service:

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and server functions (data centre in Singapore).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and Apple Push Notification service (iPhone) deliver notifications. A chat notification contains the sender's name and up to 200 characters of the message, so these services see it in transit.
  • OpenFreeMap and Esri provide map images. Like any online map, they see the area being viewed and your IP address, but never your account or your routes.

We do not sell your personal data or share it with anyone for advertising.

5. How we protect it

  • All connections use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes; device credentials only as hashes.
  • Photos and voice messages are stored privately and shown through links that expire within an hour.
  • On your phone, your sign-in is kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore, and your route history in protected storage that is not backed up.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Routes stay until you delete your account. You can remove the history stored on your phone at any time in Settings → Delete local history. When you delete your account, your data is deleted as described below. Abuse reports and manager access logs may be kept after an account is deleted, without the account attached, to keep the service safe.

7. Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account and enter your password. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, routes, profile photo, devices and push tokens, and erases the messages, photos and voice messages you sent from everyone's chats. Groups you own are passed to another member. If you can't use the app, see Delete your account to ask us by email.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: view and edit your details in Profile.
  • Export: export any day's route as a GPX file.
  • Delete: delete your account in the app, or ask us.
  • Control: choose who can message or call you, whether others see when you were last online, and whether read receipts are sent.

To make any other request about your data, email softwaredevrk@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

9. Age

My Journey is for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
