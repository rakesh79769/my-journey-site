Delete your My Journey account

My Journey, by Rakesh Kumar

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open My Journey and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete account.

Your account is deleted immediately, and the app signs out.

Without the app

Email softwaredevrk@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my My Journey account". We confirm it's you, then delete the account within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account details: email, name, username, phone number and password.
  • All routes uploaded from your phones.
  • Your profile photo.
  • The messages, photos and voice messages you sent. They are erased from everyone's chats and show as deleted.
  • Your chat memberships, reactions, blocks, settings and call history.
  • Your signed-in devices and notification tokens.

What is kept

  • Abuse reports, including a copy of a reported message, without your account attached, so we can keep the service safe.
  • Records of managers viewing accounts, without your account attached.
  • Messages other people sent you stay in their chats.

Route history saved on your own phone is removed when you delete your account in the app. If you delete by email, also uninstall the app or use Settings → Delete local history.
